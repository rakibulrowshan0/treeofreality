THE ROOT PROPOSITION

Reality exists before the stories we tell about it.

The Tree of Reality is an attempt to construct a continuous educational map of existence — beginning with the most fundamental questions of being and moving gradually toward nature, life, humanity, civilization, technology, the modern world and possible futures.

It is not intended to be a final classification of knowledge. It is a navigational architecture: a way to ask what came before, what depends upon what, what emerged from what, and how increasingly complex worlds became possible.

Reality → Nature → Life → Humanity → Civilization → Modern World
